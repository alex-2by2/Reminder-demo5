This Privacy Policy explains what information Master Reminder App ("the App," "we," "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your email address (or your Google account's name and email, if you sign in with Google) and a display name. We generate a unique ID for your account to support the family-sharing features described below.

1.2 Content You Create

The App is a broad personal organizer, and depending on which features you use, the content you create may include:

  • Tasks, reminders, notes, tags, and subtasks
  • Habit tracking data and streaks
  • Mood and sleep logs
  • Journal entries
  • Financial records: expenses, income, budgets, bills, EMIs, investments, recurring expenses, and savings goals
  • Khata Book (ledger) entries — including names, phone numbers, and amounts owed for people you add as contacts in this feature. If you use this feature, you are providing us with personal information about other individuals, not just yourself. Please only use it for contacts who are aware you're tracking this information.
  • Student data: subjects, exam dates, attendance records
  • Medicine names, doses, and reminder schedules
  • Vehicle logs, warranty records, and shopping/packing lists
  • Shift schedules, subscriptions, birthdays, and life events you record
  • Photos you choose to attach to tasks

1.3 Optional Device Features

  • Location: if you tap "attach location" on a task, we request your device's current GPS coordinates once, to generate a Google Maps link saved with that task. We do not track your location in the background or at any other time.
  • Microphone: if you use voice commands, your speech is processed using your browser's built-in speech recognition (commonly provided by Google when using Chrome), which may send audio to that provider's servers to produce a text transcript. We do not separately store voice recordings.
  • Photo library / camera: if you attach an image to a task, we access the specific photo you select.

1.4 Data Shared With Other Users

Certain features are designed to share information with people you choose:

  • Family Task Sharing: sharing a task with someone by email sends that task's title, notes, and timing to the recipient's account.
  • Shared Workspaces: tasks added to a shared workspace are visible to everyone who has joined that workspace using its invite code.
  • Leaderboard: your display name and habit-tracking score are visible to other users of the App in the leaderboard feature (your financial, health, journal, and other private data are never included in this — see Section 4).

1.5 AI Features

Features like AI task suggestions, auto-categorization, the AI chat assistant, and smart import send the relevant text (e.g., a task description, or text you paste in) to Google's Gemini AI service through our own server, so that Google never receives your account's direct identity alongside the request beyond what is inherent in the API call. See Section 3 for how this data is used.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and sync the App's features across your devices
  • To power AI-assisted features you choose to use
  • To enable the family-sharing and workspace features you set up
  • To send you reminders and notifications you've configured
  • To maintain basic security (e.g., rate-limiting to prevent abuse of AI features)
  • To improve the App's reliability (e.g., understanding crash reports, if error tracking is enabled)

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your content to train AI models.

3. Third Parties We Use

The App is built on the following service providers, each of which processes data on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments:

  • Firebase (Google): authentication, cloud data storage/sync (Firestore), and the server-side functions that power AI features and rate-limiting.
  • Google Gemini AI: processes text you submit through AI features, via our server.
  • OpenWeatherMap: if you add your own API key to use the weather widget, your city queries are sent directly to OpenWeatherMap from your device.
  • Google Translate: powers the in-app language switcher.

4. Data Storage & Security

Most of your data is stored locally on your device (via browser local storage). If you're signed in, your data also syncs to Google Cloud Firestore so it's available across your devices. Access to your cloud data is restricted by server-side security rules so that, with the exception of the limited leaderboard/lookup fields described in Section 1.4, only you can read or write your own stored data. Data is transmitted over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
